Here are the three foundational pillars you must implement immediately to stop the bleed and reclaim your market share:

1

1. Shift from Self-Taught to a “Paint-by-Numbers” 11-Step Sales System

  • • The Revenue Leak: Relying on sales reps to use their “natural talent” or ad-lib presentations creates massive inconsistencies, causing warm leads to slip through the cracks.
  • • The Grosso Strategy: Implement a strict, replicable 11-Step Sales Process. When sales reps follow a structured, predictable roadmap, they do not have to think about what to say next. This allows them to focus 100% on the customer, making the close feel like a natural conclusion.
2

2. Master the “A.I.M.” Method for Objection Handling

  • • The Revenue Leak: Sales reps often try to counter or argue with a prospect's initial reflex objections (e.g., “The price is too high” or “I need three estimates”), which immediately triggers defensive behavior.
  • • The Grosso Strategy: Train your team to use the A.I.M. Methodology (Acknowledge, Ignore, and Move on). By acknowledging the objection without pushing back, you lower the prospect's defenses. This shifts the focus back to building value before formal price delivery.
3

3. Elevate Sales Managers into Active Sales Coaches

  • • The Revenue Leak: Many companies treat sales managers like administrative supervisors who simply track KPIs rather than actively developing the team.
  • • The Grosso Strategy: True sales growth requires building a Culture of Accountability. Sales leaders must lead by example by participating in sales calls, leading role-play sessions, and actively coaching reps on specific pipeline bottlenecks to maintain consistent team performance.
